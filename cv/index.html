Profile

I come at it from two angles: solid production Python on one side, and modeling instincts from a PhD in Scientific ML and a Master's in Applied Mathematics on the other. I write clean, well-tested code, and I care most about ML that's reliable and interpretable enough to actually ship, not just work once in a notebook.
