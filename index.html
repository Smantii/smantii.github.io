About

Research rigor,
production mindset.

I’m a Machine Learning Engineer. I build machine learning systems from start to finish: the messy data and feature work up front, then training, validation, and getting models running on high-performance compute.

Most recently I was a Machine Learning Engineer intern at Compular, where I owned the ML pipelines that predict material and formulation properties for their computational materials simulation platform. Before that I did my PhD in Scientific Machine Learning in the Computational Physics & Machine Learning Lab at Aarhus University, and stayed on as a Research Assistant. Along the way I was a Visiting Scholar at Virginia Tech. My background is in mathematics: a BSc and an MSc in Applied Mathematics from Università di Pisa.

I come at ML from two angles: solid production Python on one side, and the modeling rigor of a PhD and a mathematics background on the other. In practice that means clean, tested code, careful evaluation, and models that hold up outside the notebook. My research was on symbolic regression and interpretable models, backed by open-source JAX libraries; lately I’ve also been building LLM agents with tool calling, planning, reflection and evals.

After several years of research and work abroad, I’m now open to new ML roles. When I’m not working, you’ll find me bouldering, at the gym or on a tennis court, or deep into music and movies.
